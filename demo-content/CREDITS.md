# Demo image credits

All PNG illustrations in images/ are generated by bin/generate-demo-images.py
using only Python's standard library. They contain abstract animal shapes in the
kit's blue, gold, cream, paper and ink palette. The 12 illustrations show ten
dogs and two cats, using seated, walking, playing and resting poses, pointed
and floppy ears, patches and varied body shapes. They depict no real animal or person.

The generated illustrations are released under CC0 1.0 Universal. You may copy,
change and use them without asking permission or giving credit.

The script writes only IHDR, IDAT and IEND chunks. It adds no EXIF, text, GPS or
other identifying metadata. Run it from the repository with:

```sh
python3 -I bin/generate-demo-images.py
```

The 12 fictional practice profiles in sample.json are separate from the
edge-case source fixtures used by tests. Their fees are sample amounts shown
in the site currency. No profile, story or testimonial describes a real animal
or adoption. Demo imports track their own records for removal.
